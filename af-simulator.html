About this model

A monodomain simulation with the Fenton-Karma three-variable cell model runs on the surface of a stylised left atrium with four pulmonary veins, the appendage and the mitral annulus. Fibrillation is not scripted: it arises from the tissue properties you set.

It is a teaching model. The wall has no thickness or fibre direction, there is no right atrium, the upstroke is broader than in real tissue, and the ventricular complexes are a template driven by a simple AV node rule. Numbers are indicative, not patient-specific.
